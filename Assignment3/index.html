Summary

The Last of Us, a game created by Naughty Dog, is a game about a dystopian, apocalyptic universe. This universe focuses on the life of Joel Miller and Ellie. Joel and Ellie travel across the country to transport her to Saint Mary’s Hospital in Washington to find a cure to the infection that has devastated the country. Ellie is the only known immune person thus far.

Chapter 1: Hometown

This is the first chapter where we are introduced to Joel. The chapter starts off in a regular house, where we see a daughter and father duo. This duo does not stay normal long, and quickly Sarah, Joel’s daughter, discovers that her dad is gone and has not come back from the previous night after he left a note. Joel returns, running into the house, explaining that there is something wrong and to not go outside. They end up leaving the house after a tussle with their infected neighbor, and then meet Joel’s brother, Tommy. They exit the city, but not without losing Sarah along the way.

Chapter 2: Quarantine Zone (Summer)

We are introduced to Ellie at this point, as well as Tess, who convinces Joel to come with her to do a smuggle operation for the group known as the Fireflies. The Fireflies are one of the first groups to band together, and suspect they can create a cure for the infected after they find that Ellie was bitten and survived. Joel and Tess make their way to where they need to go and are immediately stopped. They lose Tess on the way, and Joel and Ellie are forced to continue on.

Chapter 3: The Outskirts (Summer)

Ellie and Joel continue their journey. They make their way to a friend of Joel’s named Bill, who eventually ends up providing them with transport. Joel and Ellie’s relationship begins to grow from strangers to more of a father-daughter duo, and Joel refuses to talk about his loss of Tess or his daughter, Sarah. When they reach Bill’s town, they arrive to find it completely filled with traps, and when they find him, Bill cuffs Ellie to a metal bar. Ellie rips the metal bar out of the wall, and Joel steps in the middle, explaining that Bill owes him, and they need a ride. Bill reluctantly agrees, and they all set off to find a car battery in a military vehicle that is lodged in the local school building.
